# English

## 现有词汇

### zeitgeist
- **词义**: the defining spirit or mood of a particular period of history
- **中文**: 时代精神，时代思潮
- **例句**: His art captures the **zeitgeist** of the 21st century.
- **例句**: The novel perfectly reflects the **zeitgeist** of post-war America.

### resilient
- **词义**: able to recover quickly from difficulties; tough
- **中文**: 有韧性的，坚韧不拔的
- **用法**: You must be **resilient** in the face of adversity.
- **例句**: Children are often more **resilient** than adults give them credit for.
- **例句**: The economy proved remarkably **resilient** after the crisis.
- **词形变化**: resilience (n.), resiliently (adv.)

### reference
- **词义**: the act of mentioning someone or something
- **中文**: 提及，参考，引用
- **用法**: There is no further **reference** to him in her diary.
- **词组**: in reference to (关于), with reference to (参照), reference book (参考书)
- **例句**: The report made no **reference** to the earlier study.

## 常考语法要点 (Grammar Tips)

### 冠词 (Articles) — 常见错误

- **特指 vs 泛指**: "I love **the** music" (特指某音乐) vs "I love **music**" (泛指音乐)
- **抽象名词**: "**Honesty** is important." (不加 the) — 除非特指: "**The honesty** of his answer impressed me."
- **乐器**: play **the** piano / play **the** guitar (加 the)
- **球类/运动**: play **basketball** / play **chess** (不加 the)
- **独一无二**: **the** sun, **the** moon, **the** internet

### 介词 (Prepositions) — 高频易错

- **depend on** (不是 depend with/in)
- **different from** (英式: different to; 美式: different than)
- **interested in** (不是 interested on)
- **capable of** (不是 capable to)
- **comply with** (不是 comply to)
- **compare to** (比作) vs **compare with** (与…比较)
- **concerned about** (担心) vs **concerned with** (与…有关)

### 虚拟语气 (Subjunctive Mood)

- **与现在事实相反**: If I **were** you, I would... (不是 I was)
- **与过去事实相反**: If I **had known**, I **would have** come.
- **建议/要求类动词后**: I suggest that he **leave** (不是 leaves) immediately.

### 主谓一致 (Subject-Verb Agreement)

- **Each / Every / Either / Neither** + 单数动词: Every student **has** (不是 have) to submit.
- **A number of** + 复数: A number of issues **have** arisen.
- **The number of** + 单数: The number of cases **is** increasing.
- **Not only A but also B** → 动词随 B: Not only the teacher but also the students **agree**.

### 时态 (Tenses) — 关键区别

- **I have lived here for 5 years.** (现在仍住)
- **I lived here for 5 years.** (现在已经不住)
- **I have been living here since 2020.** (强调持续)
- **By next year, I will have lived here for 5 years.** (将来完成时)

### 常见语法误区

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|---------|---------|
| I look forward **to hear** from you. | I look forward **to hearing** from you. |
| **Although** it rained, **but** we went. | **Although** it rained, we went. |
| The reason is **because**... | The reason is **that**... |
| I **am used to** work hard. | I **am used to working** hard. |
| **Between** you and **I** | **Between** you and **me** |

## 易混淆词 (Confusing Words)

| Word Pair | Distinction | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **affect / effect** | v.影响 / n.影响(后果) | The weather **affected** my mood. / The **effect** was immediate. |
| **compliment / complement** | v.赞美 / v.补充 | She **complimented** my work. / The wine **complements** the dish. |
| **principal / principle** | n.校长/adj.主要的 / n.原则 | The **principal** reason... / Stick to your **principles**. |
| **stationary / stationery** | adj.静止的 / n.文具 | The car remained **stationary**. / I need to buy **stationery**. |
| **disinterested / uninterested** | adj.公正的 / adj.不感兴趣的 | A judge must be **disinterested**. / He was **uninterested** in the lecture. |
| **imply / infer** | v.暗示(说话者) / v.推断(听者) | What are you **implying**? / I **inferred** from his tone that... |
| **historic / historical** | adj.有历史意义的 / adj.历史上的 | a **historic** event (重大事件) / a **historical** novel (历史小说) |
| **economic / economical** | adj.经济的 / adj.节约的 | **economic** growth (经济增长) / an **economical** car (省油的车) |

## 写作技巧 (Writing Tips)

### 段落衔接词 (Transition Words)

| 功能 | 词语 |
|------|------|
| 补充信息 | furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally |
| 对比转折 | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, conversely |
| 因果关系 | therefore, consequently, thus, as a result, accordingly |
| 举例说明 | for instance, for example, such as, to illustrate |
| 总结归纳 | in conclusion, to summarize, overall, in brief |
| 强调 | indeed, in fact, notably, particularly, significantly |

### 句子多样性 (Sentence Variety)

- **简单句**: The experiment failed.
- **复合句 (Compound)**: The experiment failed, **so** we revised the approach.
- **复杂句 (Complex)**: **Although** the experiment failed, it provided valuable insights.
- **以分词开头**: **Having reviewed** the data, the team proposed a new model.
- **以介词短语开头**: **In light of recent findings**, the policy must be updated.

## 学习资源与建议 (Resources & Tips)

### 推荐学习资源

- **词典**: Merriam-Webster (英英), Cambridge Dictionary, Collins, Longman
- **阅读**: The Economist, The New York Times, The Guardian
- **听力**: BBC Learning English, NPR, TED Talks, VOA Special English
- **单词**: Anki (间隔重复), Vocabulary.com, Quizlet
- **语法**: *English Grammar in Use* (Raymond Murphy), Grammarly
- **口语**: ELSA Speak, italki, HelloTalk

### 学习建议

1. **每天15分钟** — 短期高频优于每周一次长时间学习
2. **间隔重复 (Spaced Repetition)** — 用 Anki 或类似工具复习单词
3. **主动输出 (Active Recall)** — 不要只读，要默写和造句
4. **沉浸 (Immersion)** — 看美剧（先关字幕再看英文字幕再关字幕）
5. **影子跟读 (Shadowing)** — 模仿母语者的语调、连读和重音
6. **阅读分级** — i+1 原则：读比你当前水平略难的材料
7. **记录错题** — 建立自己的"常见错误"笔记本

### 常见词根词缀 (Word Roots & Affixes)

- **-logy** (学科): biology, psychology, sociology
- **pre-** (之前): preview, predict, precede
- **re-** (再次/回): review, return, reconsider
- **-able/-ible** (能够): manageable, feasible, credible
- **un-** (否定): uncertain, unprecedented, unavoidable
- **-ify** (使…化): simplify, clarify, verify
- **-tion/-sion** (名词后缀): education, conclusion, expansion
- **anti-** (反对): anti-war, antibody, anticlimax
- **mal-** (坏的): malfunction, malpractice, malnutrition
- **bene-** (好的): benefit, benevolent, beneficial

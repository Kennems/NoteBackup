# Word memorization

### aerosol

n. 雾化器，悬浮微粒

He took out an aerosol can and sprayed paint over the shop’s outside wall.

### inversion

n. 倒置，颠倒，倒转

His account of the details of the case was an inversion of the facts.

### characterization

n. 塑造，描述方法。界定方法

The professor’s books have good characterization, and lively dialogue.

### bacterial

In ancient times, people often died from a bacterial infection.

### capillary

The pathogen would cause the capillaries to become leaky.
